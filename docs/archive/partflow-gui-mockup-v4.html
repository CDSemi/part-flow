    Priority Management — Hot PO Demand

    Priority belongs to PO Demand, ranked per Department. Drag to reorder, ✕ to remove — changes apply immediately and are audited. New Hot entries are added at the bottom. Multiple POs for the same PN may hold different priorities.
    Hot Part is a label on top of priority_rank — it never replaces it. Allocation & work ordering: ① Hot rank ② earliest due date — a stable deterministic tie-breaker is an implementation detail, not a business rule (§18).

    Add PO Demand to Hot list

    Search by PN, PO or Job Number and select — or scan the PN barcode with this dialog open.
    Demo barcode: PF:PN:1014 (PF-SHAFT-014) — Enter adds it directly. If a PN has multiple active PO Demands, each is listed separately.
